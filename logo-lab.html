VECTRA / IDENTITY STUDY 01

Six directions for the next logo.

Each option keeps the identity precise and digital without defaulting to generic developer-tool branding. The cobalt is a signal, not the whole personality.

Click any concept to isolate it.
01 / EVOLUTION

Refined Bracket

The safest evolution. It preserves recognition while making the mark cleaner, quieter, and less code-literal.

Best for: continuity
02 / RECOMMENDED

Vector Point

A directional V ending in a precise node. It suggests movement, decisions, and a clear destination without feeling overly technical.

Best for: distinctiveness
03 / STRUCTURAL

Open Frame

A V inside an intentionally incomplete viewport. It connects the identity to websites while keeping the symbol abstract.

Best for: web focus
04 / SYSTEMATIC

Axis

A precise intersection and offset point. It feels engineered and adaptable, with a strong app-icon silhouette.

Best for: systems thinking
05 / EXPRESSIVE

Fold

Two planes form a V with a subtle dimensional shift. More visual personality, while remaining simple enough for small sizes.

Best for: creative energy
06 / EDITORIAL

Word Signal

A typography-first direction with one sharp signal embedded in the name. Mature, minimal, and easy to pair with project imagery.

Best for: understated premium
